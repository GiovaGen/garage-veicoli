Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short. Garage Veicoli needs no account and sends none of your data to any server. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no ads, no usage analytics and no tracking.

1. Who we are

App owner: [OWNER FULL NAME], [ADDRESS]. Contact for any privacy question: garage.veicoli@docklab.app.

2. What data the app handles

To work, the app stores the data you enter: vehicles (name, type, plate, year and similar), mileage readings, deadlines, maintenance records, favorite places and notes. This data is saved only in your device's storage, in a local database.

We have no access to this data: it is not sent to us or to third parties.

3. What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising.
  • No usage analytics, statistics or tracking tools.
  • No collection of your location, contacts or other phone data.

4. Notifications

Deadline alerts are local notifications: they are created and shown by your device, without going through a server. The app asks for permission to send notifications; you can revoke it at any time in the system settings.

5. Exporting and importing your data

From Settings you can export your data to a file and import it again. The file is created only when you ask for it, and you save or share it wherever you choose. The file is not encrypted: keep it as carefully as you would a personal document.

6. Third-party services

The app is distributed through Google Play and the App Store, which process data about downloads and any purchases under their own policies. We do not receive your payment details.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on the device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app, which removes it. Your phone's operating system (for example Android backups or iCloud) may include the app's data in device backups, according to the settings you chose.

8. Your rights

Since the data stays on your device, you control it directly: you can view, edit, export and delete it in the app. For any other request write to garage.veicoli@docklab.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

9. Future changes

If the app later adds online services (for example email alerts or sync between devices), we will update this policy before release, stating which data is processed and why, and ask for your consent where required.
